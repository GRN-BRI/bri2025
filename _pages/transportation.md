# Transportation
Tangshan offers outstanding multimodal connectivityserving both domestic and international attendeesthrough integrated air and rail networks.

## INTERNATIONAL AIR ACCESS
THREE MAJOR INTERNATIONAL GATEWAYS PROVIDE GLOBAL ACCESS:
- BEIJING DAXING INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (PKX),DISTANCE: 190 KM, DRIVING TIME: ABOUT 2 HOURS.
- BEIJING CAPITAL INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (PEK),DISTANCE: 180 KM, DRIVING TIME: ABOUT 2 HOURS.
- TIANJIN BINHAI INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (TSN):DISTANCE: 120 KM, DRIVING TIME: ABOUT 1.5 HOURS.

### Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK)
- Route 1: PEK→Beijing Railway Station
- Route 2:Beijing Railway Station→Tangshan Railway Station
#### Route 1 Explanation:

**Subway [28 CNY]**

Terminal 3 →Terminal 2→Dongzhimen (Transfer)→Beijing Railway Station

(Terminal 3 Subway Operating Hours: 06:20-22:50)
(Terminal 2 Subway Operating Hours: 06:35-23:10)
(Dongzhimen Subway Operating Hours: 05:13-23:13)

**E-Hailing [60-100 CNY]**

Terminal 2: Go to the southeast side of the 1st floor of T2 Parking Building for pickup.
Terminal 3: Go to Area H on the B2 floor of T3 Parking Building for pickup.

**Taxi**

Terminal 2:
Pick-up point: Middle lane outside Gates 5–9 on the 1st floor.

Terminal 3:
Pick-up point: Taxi waiting area on B1 floor.

#### Route 2 Explanation:
**High-speed train[80-110 CNY]**

You can register and buy tickets on the Railway 12306 APP. You need to present your passport for both registration and ticket inspection when entering the station.


### Beijing Daxing International Airport (PKX)
- Route 1: PEK→Beijing Railway Station
- Route 2:Beijing Railway Station→Tangshan Railway Station
#### Route 1 Explanation:

**Subway [40 CNY]**

Daxing Airport→Caoqiao
Caoqiao→Jishuitan (Transfer)→Beijing Railway Station
Caoqiao→Jiaomen Xi(W)(Transfer)→Beijing Railway Station
(Caoqiao Subway Operating Hours: 05:41-22:30)
(Daxing Airport Subway Operating Hours: 06:00-23:00)

**E-Hailing [60-100 CNY]**

Go to the 1M mezzanine of the P1 Parking Building for pickup.

**Taxi**

Pick-up Point: East side of the Middle Edge Road on the 1st floor of the Terminal Building, near Gates 18 and 20.

The taxi waiting area has two platforms: one for Beijing-bound taxis and the other for Hebei-bound taxis. The Beijing-bound platforms are on the east and west sides, with the Hebei-bound platform in the middle.

#### Route 2 Explanation:
**High-speed train[80-110 CNY]**

You can register and buy tickets on the Railway 12306 APP. You need to present your passport for both registration and ticket inspection when entering the station.


### Tianjin Binhai International Airport(TSN)
- Route 1: TSN→Tianjin Railway Station
- Route 2:Tianjin Railway Station→Tangshan Railway Station
#### Route 1 Explanation:

**Subway [3 CNY]**

Binhai International Airport → Tianjin Railway Station
(Binhai International Airport Subway Operating Hours: 06:00-22:54)

**E-Hailing [30-50 CNY]**

Pick-up Point: Located on the east side of Island A in the Traffic Center of the Public Parking Lot.

**Taxi**

Pick-up Point: Located outside Gates 5-6 of the Arrival Hall on the 1st floor of the Terminal Building.

#### Route 2 Explanation:
**High-speed train[40-60 CNY]**

You can register and buy tickets on the Railway 12306 APP. You need to present your passport for both registration and ticket inspection when entering the station.

**Traffic Tip:** Subway tickets can be purchased at self-service ticket machines, which support WeChat Pay, Alipay, cash, as well as Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay card payments.
